# Estrada Landscaping — short brand film

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Thoughtful local care makes the outside of home feel like home.
- **Identity:** Use the site's actual deep garden green, soft mineral cream, sage, and warm clay; Newsreader-like editorial display serif paired with DM Sans-like human sans.
- **Hero:** The neighborhood yard itself, transformed by detail and framed as a place to live.
- **Motion:** Macro-to-wide camera pullbacks, sharp masked typography, and circle/line forms that grow from garden edging.
- **Arc:** A calm but immediate invitation accelerates into the breadth of the work, pauses for trust and local proof, and resolves into a clear contact lockup.
- **Payoff:** A clay sun-disc echoes the opening's local-root seal, then becomes its match-cut carrier; no sales-button artwork.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, fixed 1920×1080 composition.
- **Audience/takeaway:** Homeowners near Coatesville; speak directly with a local crew for practical outdoor care.
